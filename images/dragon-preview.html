Blue lightning dragon

Three transparent SVG tiles. Sapphire armor, icy horns, and branching white lightning.

Connected tiles

Game sizes · 24, 32, and 48 pixels per tile

Head orientations

Body bends

Normal and reversed bend shading blend to match each direction of travel. Both share the same white lightning path.

Using the assets

Each file uses viewBox="0 0 100 100" and faces right. Place the tail to the left of the body and the head to its right, with tile edges touching. Skin connects at y=28–72; lightning meets at y=50. Rotate around the tile center for up/down and mirror horizontally for left.

The active src/Game.elm game embeds all five dragon SVG files as Base64 data URLs, including both bend variants. It is self-contained for Ellie and does not fetch image files. After editing an SVG, update its corresponding embedded image constant in Elm.
